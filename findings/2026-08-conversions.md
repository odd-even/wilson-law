# Findings — Form & Button Conversions

**Period:** Jul 27 – Aug 25, 2026  
**Source:** Squarespace Analytics → Engagement → Form & Button Conversions  
**Asset:** `_assets/squarespace/2026-08-25_form-button-conversions.png`

## KPIs

| Metric | Value | MoM |
|--------|------:|-----|
| Form submissions | **10** | **−38%** |
| Button clicks | **37** | **−12%** |

## Form detail (by page)

| Page | Submissions | Unique views | Conversion rate |
|------|------------:|-------------:|----------------:|
| `/contact` | 10 | 76 | **13.2%** |

All tracked form submissions in this period came from the Contact page. Submissions are sparse (0 most days; peaks of 1–2).

## Interpretation

- **10 form leads / 30 days** ≈ ~0.3 per day — modest but real for a credibility-focused transactional firm (not lead-gen volume).
- Contact unique views **76** vs Contact pageviews **101** (from Site Content) — different metrics (unique vs total views); **13.2%** form CVR on unique views is solid for a law-firm contact form.
- MoM form drop (−38%) is a small-sample swing (prior period implied ~16 submissions). Don’t overreact on one month — flag and watch next period.
- Button clicks (37, −12%) likely include “Meet with an Attorney” / “Schedule a Meeting” CTAs sitewide — useful funnel signal above the form.
