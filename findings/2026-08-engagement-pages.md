## Interpretation

- **Home dominates** (~59% of views) — expected for a small brochure site; CTA clarity on home matters most.
- **Practice Areas** gets solid secondary traffic and better exit rate (50%) — people continue exploring more often than from About.
- **About Us** has the **longest time on page (2m 27s)** but **very high bounce (93.55%)** — visitors who land there engage the bios, then often leave (or arrive as single-page sessions).
- **Contact** ~11% of views — healthy share for a conversion page on this scale.
- No evidence of `/cart` or other utility pages in “pages with views” for this period (good).
