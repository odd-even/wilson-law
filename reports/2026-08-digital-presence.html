We’d like your approval before changing the disclaimer on the Contact page. The site is served over HTTPS — an encrypted connection. Below is the current wording and the proposed replacement.

Current copy

Please note that any information provided to Wilson Law Firm through this website is not considered confidential as this site is not secure. Please do not submit confidential or sensitive information through this website. Contacting Wilson Law Firm through this website or by email does not create an attorney-client relationship. Such a relationship is established only after the firm has confirmed its agreement to represent you.
